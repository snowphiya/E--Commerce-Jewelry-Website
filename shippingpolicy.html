Shipping Policy

Order Processing

Orders are usually processed within 1–3 business days after payment confirmation.

Delivery

We currently deliver across India. Estimated delivery time is 3–7 business days after shipping.

Shipping Charges

Shipping charges, if applicable, will be displayed at checkout before you complete your order. Any free-shipping offers will be clearly mentioned on the website or during promotional periods.

Order Tracking

Once your order has been shipped, you may receive tracking information through your registered contact details, where available.

Delays

Delivery may occasionally be delayed due to weather conditions, courier issues, public holidays, remote locations, or other circumstances beyond our control.

Incorrect Address

Please make sure your shipping address and contact details are correct before placing your order. Iyan Jewels is not responsible for delays or additional charges caused by an incorrect or incomplete address.

Damaged Package

If your package appears damaged when delivered, please contact us as soon as possible with photographs/videos of the package and the product.

Contact Us

For any shipping-related questions, please contact Iyan Jewels through our official contact channels.
